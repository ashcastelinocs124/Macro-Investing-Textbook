Economies do not run on their own. Two sets of decision-makers steer them, and every macro investor watches both:

1. **[Central banks & monetary policy](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/04-central-banks/).** How central banks set the price of money, and how a rate change reaches your loan.
2. **[Fiscal policy & government debt](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/05-fiscal-policy/).** How governments use taxes and spending, and when debt becomes dangerous.

**Central banks by country.** [Federal Reserve](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/central-banks/federal-reserve/), [European Central Bank](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/central-banks/european-central-bank/), [Bank of Japan](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/central-banks/bank-of-japan/), [Reserve Bank of India](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/central-banks/reserve-bank-of-india/) and [People's Bank of China](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-2-policy/central-banks/peoples-bank-of-china/): what each bank is, how it decides, and every tool it uses.
