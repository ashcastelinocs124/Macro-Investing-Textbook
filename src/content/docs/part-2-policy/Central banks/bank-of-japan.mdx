## What it is

The **Bank of Japan** (BoJ) was founded in 1882 as Japan's central bank ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/about/outline/index.htm)). The current law dates from 1998, after a full revision of the 1942 Act, and rests on two principles: respect for the Bank's autonomy, and transparency about its policy and operations ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/about/outline/history/index.htm)). It aims for 2% inflation, a target set in January 2013 ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/outline/index.htm)). For the goal chart, see the [Japan page](/Macro-Investing-Textbook/part-1-economy/five-big-economies/japan/).

## Who decides

- **The Policy Board** is the Bank's highest decision-making body. It sets the guideline for currency and monetary control ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/about/organization/policyboard/)).
- It has nine members: the Governor, two Deputy Governors and six other members. Decisions are made by majority vote ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/outline/index.htm)).
- It holds eight Monetary Policy Meetings a year, each lasting two days ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/outline/index.htm)).

## The tools

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Overnight call rate** | The policy rate. The Bank sets a guideline for this rate, the rate at which banks lend to each other overnight ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2025/k250617a.htm)). |
| **Interest on current account balances** | The rate the Bank pays on the reserves banks hold with it. A new rate took effect on 21 March 2024 ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2024/k240319a.htm)). |
| **Government bond purchases** | The Bank buys Japanese government bonds, and is reducing its monthly purchases step by step ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2025/k250617a.htm)). |
| **Past unconventional tools** | Large-scale easing with yield curve control, and a negative interest rate. In March 2024 the Bank ended both, judging that they had fulfilled their roles ([Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/mopo/mpmdeci/state_2024/k240319a.htm)). |

## Key takeaways

- The BoJ is one of the oldest central banks in the group (1882), with a **nine-member Policy Board** that votes **eight times a year**.
- Its policy rate is the **overnight call rate**. It also buys **government bonds**, at a shrinking pace.
- It used **yield curve control and negative rates** for years, and ended both in **March 2024**.
